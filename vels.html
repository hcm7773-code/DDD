PROJECT 02 · CASE STUDY

VELS: Virtue Education Learning System

AI-Assisted Character & Virtue Education Platform Grounded in Kohlberg's Moral Psychology

Executive Summary

Traditional character education frequently relies on passive didactic instruction and rigid dogmatism, neglecting the nuanced sociocultural dilemmas students face. VELS models Kohlberg's Six Stages of Moral Development and Rest's Four-Component Model. It embeds learners in interactive branching narratives and prompts Socratic reflection journals to cultivate genuine socio-emotional learning (SEL) competencies.

PILLAR 01

Moral Dilemma Branching

Simulates authentic ethical dilemmas across peer integrity, institutional fairness, digital privacy, and ecological equity.

PILLAR 02

Socratic Mentorship Constraints

The AI mentor is architected with strict anti-preaching guardrails; it only poses dialectical counter-questions that stimulate meta-reflection.

PILLAR 03

SEL Competency Radar

Tracks longitudinal growth across self-awareness, social empathy, responsible decision-making, and constructive conflict resolution.

PILLAR 04

Anthropological Reflexivity

Draws upon ethnographic reflexive writing to empower students to articulate the deeper values governing their decisions.
