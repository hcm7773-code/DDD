PROJECT 03 · CASE STUDY

Educational AI Agent Ecosystem

Multi-Agent Architecture for Collaborative, Autonomous Pedagogical Scaffolding

Executive Summary

Single-prompt chatbots fail in educational settings because they prematurely reveal final answers, lack longitudinal tracking of cognitive misconceptions, and cannot balance empathy with academic rigor. The Educational AI Agent Ecosystem introduces a specialized multi-agent swarm architecture where discrete agents—Socratic Tutors, Cognitive Diagnosticians, and Ethical Guardians—collaborate in real time across a shared cognitive blackboard.

AGENT ROLE 01

Socratic Tutor Agent

Forbidden from outputting turnkey solutions; asks step-by-step counter-questions to induce conceptual self-discovery.

AGENT ROLE 02

Cognitive Diagnostician

Identifies underlying student misconceptions in STEM (e.g., fraction division misconceptions, sign errors, force balance fallacies).

AGENT ROLE 03

Cultural Contextualizer

Translates abstract concepts into relatable cultural narratives and everyday real-world examples, enhancing learning engagement.

AGENT ROLE 04

Safety & Ethics Guardian

Maintains strict classroom boundaries, detects learner anxiety or distress, and flags alerts for human educator intervention.
