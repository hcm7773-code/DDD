PROJECT 01 · CASE STUDY

GAEC: Global AI English Certification

AI-based Adaptive English Assessment Platform Aligned with CEFR Standards

Executive Summary

Traditional standardized language assessments suffer from high human grading latency, inter-rater variance, and non-actionable score readouts. GAEC solves these challenges by combining multimodal acoustic speech feature analysis with strict rubric-constrained LLM evaluation pipelines, generating instantaneous diagnostic CEFR proficiency feedback (A1–C2).

PILLAR 01

Acoustic Speech Alignment

Measures syllable-per-minute articulation rates, atypical pause durations, prosodic pitch contours, and phoneme intelligibility.

PILLAR 02

Lexical & Syntactic Depth

Calculates Academic Word List (AWL) density, type-token ratio, dependency parse-tree depth, and subordinate clause ratios.

PILLAR 03

Anti-Hallucination Rubrics

Enforces structured JSON Schema validation where every assessment claim must cite verbatim textual and acoustic timestamps.

PILLAR 04

Actionable Scaffolding

Generates precision micro-remediation exercises targeting specific phonological or syntactic obstacles.
